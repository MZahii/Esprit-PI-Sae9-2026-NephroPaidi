Privacy Policy

NephrosPaidi is committed to protecting the privacy of children, parents, and healthcare professionals. This policy explains how we collect, store, and use personal and medical data within our digital healthcare platform.

1. Introduction

When you use NephrosPaidi, you trust us with sensitive medical and personal information. We take this responsibility seriously and apply strong security and privacy practices.

This Privacy Policy helps you understand what information we collect, why we collect it, and how we protect and manage your data.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information to provide better services to our users. The types of information we collect include:

2.1 Information You Provide

When you create an account or use our services, you provide us with personal information that includes:

  • Patient name, age, and medical history
  • Parent/guardian contact information
  • Doctor professional information
  • Login credentials
  • Appointment details
  • Lab results and medical reports

2.2 Automatic Information

We automatically collect and store certain information when you use our services:

  • Device and browser information
  • Platform usage statistics
  • Login activity logs
  • System performance data

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to provide, maintain, and improve our services. Specifically, we use your information to:

  • Manage patient medical records
  • Schedule and manage appointments
  • Assist doctors in monitoring kidney diseases
  • Provide AI-based risk predictions
  • Improve system performance and security
  • Send medical alerts and notifications

4. Information Sharing and Disclosure

We do not share personal information with companies, organizations, or individuals outside of our company except in the following cases:

4.1 With Your Consent

We only share patient data with doctors and authorized medical staff with explicit consent from parents or guardians.

4.2 For Legal Reasons

We will share personal information if we have a good-faith belief that access, use, preservation, or disclosure of the information is reasonably necessary to:

  • When required by law or medical regulations
  • To protect patient safety
  • To prevent fraud or security threats

5. Data Security

We work hard to protect our users from unauthorized access to or unauthorized alteration, disclosure, or destruction of information we hold. In particular:

  • Encrypted medical data storage
  • Secure login authentication
  • Restricted access for authorized doctors only
  • Regular system monitoring and security audits

6. Your Rights and Choices

You have certain rights regarding your personal information, including:

  • Access your medical data
  • Update patient information
  • Request deletion of records
  • Control who can view patient data

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the effective date at the top.

NephrosPaidi may update this Privacy Policy to improve security, comply with regulations, or add new platform features. Updates will be published on this page.
