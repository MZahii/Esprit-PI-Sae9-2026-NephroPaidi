What is pediatric kidney disease?

Pediatric kidney disease includes conditions that affect kidney function in children, such as nephrotic syndrome, chronic kidney disease, and congenital kidney disorders. Early monitoring is essential to prevent complications.

How does NephrosPaidi help monitor my child's health?

NephrosPaidi allows parents and doctors to track vital health indicators, lab results, medication schedules, and symptoms in real time. This helps detect risks early and improve treatment decisions.

Can I communicate directly with doctors through the platform?

Yes. The platform includes secure messaging, consultation scheduling, and emergency contact features to stay connected with specialists.

Is my child's medical data secure?

Yes. All medical records, reports, and health data are stored securely using protected systems that respect privacy and confidentiality standards.
