A Smart Platform for Pediatric Kidney Care

NephrosPaidi is a digital platform dedicated to pediatric nephrology, designed for both doctors and parents. It enables continuous follow-up of children with kidney diseases through secure medical records, lab tracking, and timely alerts that keep every care milestone visible.

Doctors can manage patients, review histories, and monitor key indicators. Parents can access their child’s medical information, receive guidance, and communicate with specialists for safer and faster care.

Doctor Dashboard

Manage patient profiles, monitor clinical indicators, review lab results, and follow treatment plans in one place.

Parent Portal

Access your child’s follow-up, view prescriptions and results, receive reminders, and message the care team securely.
